## Briefs behind the samples

- **B04 sample:** Focus only on coordinating pulse, playing, and rests. Prerequisite B03. Original one-note exercise; do not teach initial embouchure or demand a particular pitch. Accept when counts and silence are unambiguous and the player can assess the pattern. It is a partial illustration of B04, not the full rhythm module.
- **I01 sample:** Focus on one transition in music the reader owns or may legally use. Prerequisites B08 and basic notation. Original practice procedure; no copied score. Accept when readers can identify a section, test a change, and return to context without assuming a fixed tempo prescription.
- **R02 sample:** Explain valve notation and its limits. No playing prerequisite. Verify valve-number convention and shared fingerings with Yamaha sources. Accept when readers correctly interpret open, 1, 1–2, and 1–3. A full fingering chart is outside this sample's scope.

## Feedback to collect

Can a reader begin without rereading? Are the instructions specific enough? Does the explanation feel too sparse or too long? Where would hearing or seeing an example change understanding? Record actual feedback and revise before extracting authoring skills.
